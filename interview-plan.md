## Questions to ask
- "Tell me how you handle udhaar (credit) today."
- "What's the hardest part about billing? Why?"
- "How often do you run out of stock without realizing it?"
- "Why does that matter for your business?"
- "What have you tried to fix it?"
- "Can you show me?" (the notebook, the register, the WhatsApp group)

## Questions to avoid
- "Would you use my app?"
- "Which features would you want?"
- Yes/no questions
- Two questions at once

## Rule to remember
Talk about their business, not your idea. Ask what they did last time, not what they might do.
