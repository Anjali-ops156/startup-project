# Ideas

## Idea 1: Digital Udhaar (Credit) Ledger
- **One-line pitch:** A simple app that replaces the paper udhaar notebook so customer credit is never forgotten or disputed.
- **Business:** Karyana (kirana) stores — starting with my family's store in Ludhiana.
- **How they handle it today:** Credit given to regular customers is written in a paper notebook; entries are sometimes forgotten, which leads to confusion about how much a customer actually owes.
- **Who I'll talk to this week:** My father (our store) plus other karyana store owners I know through family and friends in Ludhiana (see `interview-plan.md`).

## Idea 2: Fast Billing + Price Lookup Tool
- **One-line pitch:** A quick billing tool with a built-in price list so totals are always correct and prices never need to be looked up manually.
- **Business:** Karyana stores.
- **How they handle it today:** Bills are totalled by hand/calculator, which sometimes causes addition mistakes; prices are recalled from memory or checked by asking someone.
- **Who I'll talk to this week:** Same set of karyana store owners (see `interview-plan.md`).

## Idea 3: Low-Stock Alert Tool
- **One-line pitch:** A tool that flags when an item is running low so the owner reorders before a customer asks for something that's out of stock.
- **Business:** Karyana stores.
- **How they handle it today:** There's no tracking system — the owner usually finds out an item is out of stock only when a customer asks, and sometimes loses that sale to another shop.
- **Who I'll talk to this week:** Same set of karyana store owners (see `interview-plan.md`).

## Idea 4: Customer Demand & Promotion Insights Tool
- **One-line pitch:** A tool that tracks which products actually sell and helps the owner send simple WhatsApp promotions or bundle offers to regular customers, instead of guessing what to discount.
- **Business:** Karyana stores.
- **How they handle it today:** No market research happens at all — there's no record of which products sell well or poorly, promotions are rare and word-of-mouth only, and customers often don't know about items the store already stocks (the "product awareness" problem my dad mentioned).
- **Who I'll talk to this week:** Same set of karyana store owners (see `interview-plan.md`).
